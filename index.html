Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

How often should a project be scanned?+
Weekly or fortnightly monitoring is generally suitable for active fit-out projects. The ideal frequency depends on the construction program, project size and the level of visibility required.
How large can a project be?+
Projects up to approximately 25,000 sq ft can be offered under the standard package, with larger projects quoted according to site size and complexity.
What do I receive after each capture?+
Depending on the selected package, deliverables can include an interactive virtual tour, point cloud, visual progress comparison, 3D/BIM comparison and a project progress report.
Can you compare my BIM or 3D model with the site?+
Yes. Where suitable BIM/3D model information is provided, the captured site can be compared with the planned model to support visualisation of planned versus executed conditions.
Do you replace a building survey or inspection?+
No. This service is designed for visual documentation and construction progress monitoring. It does not replace a licensed surveyor, engineer, building inspector or other regulated professional where their services are required.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

How often should a project be captured? +
Weekly or fortnightly monitoring is generally suitable for active fit-out projects. The ideal frequency depends on the construction program, project size and the level of visibility required.
How large can a project be? +
Projects up to approximately 25,000 sq ft can be offered under the standard package. Larger projects can be quoted according to site size, travel, complexity and required outputs.
What do I receive after each capture? +
Depending on the selected package, deliverables can include an interactive virtual tour, point cloud, visual progress comparison, project timeline, 3D/BIM comparison and visual documentation.
Can you compare BIM or 3D models with the site? +
Where suitable BIM or 3D model information is provided, captured site conditions can be compared with planned information to support visualisation of planned versus executed conditions.
Do you replace a building survey or inspection? +
No. AJ Virtual provides visual documentation and construction progress monitoring. It does not replace a licensed surveyor, engineer, building inspector or other regulated professional where their services are required.
Can I book a one-off capture? +
Yes. One-off documentation can be discussed depending on project size, location and the required outputs.
Can you work with my existing drawings or model? +
Yes. If you have suitable drawings, 3D models or BIM information, mention this in the enquiry form so the appropriate workflow can be discussed.
